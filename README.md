**Ember Isles: Duel of the Four Winds** — an original turn-based card battler. Play cards from your hand against five rivals across the isles: Pip the Sprout, Brine Mara, Cinder Fox, the Granite Warden, and the Fourfold Tempest. Four factions (Ember, Tide, Gale, Stone) each have their own colour, shape and Surge bonus — play two cards of one faction in a turn to trigger it. The foe shows its intent before it acts, so every hand is a decision: block, heal, race, or combo. Win a duel to choose a new card for your deck, heal, and sail on.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network: `game/index.html` and its modules play offline in desktop Chrome. Click a card (or press 1–9) to play it, E to end your turn, M to mute.
